# Code of conduct

MaxModel follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, patient, and respectful. Assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and focus on what's best for the people who use MaxModel. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language aren't acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, or anywhere else the project is represented.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, report it privately to the maintainer: on the repository's [Security tab](https://github.com/dhrubsingh/maxmodel/security), choose **Report a vulnerability**, which opens a private thread only maintainers can see, and say it's a conduct report. Reports are kept confidential and handled according to the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
